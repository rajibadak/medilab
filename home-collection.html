Home sample collection

A trained collector comes to your address in Egra and nearby areas for blood and other booked samples.

What happens on the visit

You book a time. The collector carries the labelled tubes, confirms your name and the tests, and draws the sample at home. The sample returns to the laboratory with the centre’s usual chain of labelling. Reports are shared the same way as a visit to the centre.

  • Trained collector and single-use supplies
  • Identity check before the sample is taken
  • Fasting rules explained when you book
  • Useful for older adults and anyone who should not travel that day
  • Available across Egra and nearby areas, confirmed at booking

Before the collector arrives

Follow the fasting instruction for your test. Keep water nearby if you are allowed to drink. Have your phone on, and keep previous reports ready if this is a repeat test. Pets should be in another room during the draw.

Home collection is for booked samples. It is not an emergency or ambulance service.
