General Diagnostics

Everyday tests and doctor visits for fever, fatigue and preventive care.

What this service covers

General diagnostics is the starting point when you are not sure which department you need. The team can book a basic panel, a doctor visit, or direct you to pathology or radiology.

Tests and studies

  • Fever and infection panels
  • Complete blood count
  • Thyroid and vitamin checks
  • Blood pressure and sugar screening
  • General medicine consultation

How to prepare

If you already have a prescription, bring it. Fasting is needed only for the tests marked on your booking slip. You can eat before a consultation that has no blood test.

Reports

Screening reports follow the laboratory schedule. The physician explains which results need a repeat test or a specialist.

Who usually books this

Adults booking a first checkup and patients who want one desk to plan the visit.

Test lists can change with the referral. The centre confirms availability, preparation and fees when you book.
