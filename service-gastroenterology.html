Gastroenterology

Laboratory and ultrasound support for digestion, liver and abdomen concerns.

What this service covers

Digestive complaints often need a mix of blood tests, stool tests and an abdomen ultrasound. A gastroenterology consultation helps decide the order of those tests. Bring previous endoscopy or ultrasound reports if you have them.

Tests and studies

  • Liver function test
  • Stool routine and occult blood
  • Abdomen ultrasound
  • Amylase and related blood tests when advised
  • Gastroenterology consultation

How to prepare

Liver tests and abdomen ultrasound are more reliable after fasting. Stool samples should be collected in the container given by the centre, not mixed with urine.

Reports

Routine liver and stool reports are usually ready within one working day. The doctor’s note is shared at the consultation.

Who usually books this

People with acidity, pain in the upper abdomen, jaundice, irregular bowels or a doctor’s referral.

Test lists can change with the referral. The centre confirms availability, preparation and fees when you book.
