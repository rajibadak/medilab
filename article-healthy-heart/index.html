Tips for a Healthy Heart

28 Aug 2025 · Cardiology

Walking, food, blood pressure and a timely ECG do more for the heart than a last-minute test after symptoms start.

Daily movement still counts when it is ordinary. A brisk walk most days of the week, less salty packaged food, and not smoking change risk more reliably than a short intense plan that stops after a fortnight.

Know your blood pressure and lipid numbers. They are quiet. People often feel fine while both are high. An ECG adds the rhythm at that moment. It does not, by itself, clear someone who has chest pain.

Family history matters when a parent or sibling had a heart attack at a younger age. Tell the cardiology desk. The tests and the consultation are planned differently when that history is known.

New chest pressure, pain spreading to the arm or jaw, sudden breathlessness or fainting is an emergency. Call for urgent care. A routine heart checkup is for planned screening, not for that hour.
