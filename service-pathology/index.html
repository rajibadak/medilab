Pathology

Blood, urine and other laboratory tests reported from the Egra centre.

What this service covers

Pathology covers the laboratory tests used to check blood counts, organ function, sugar control, thyroid balance and infection. Samples are collected at the centre or at home, then processed for a written report you can download.

Tests and studies

  • Complete blood count (CBC)
  • Blood sugar, fasting and post-meal
  • HbA1c
  • Lipid profile
  • Liver function test (LFT)
  • Kidney function test (KFT)
  • Thyroid profile
  • Urine routine and culture
  • Vitamin D and Vitamin B12

How to prepare

Many blood tests need 8 to 12 hours of fasting. Water is allowed unless the desk tells you otherwise. Carry earlier reports if this test is a follow-up. Home collection follows the same preparation rules.

Reports

Most routine reports are shared the same day or the next working day. Culture and a few specialised tests take longer. The centre will tell you the expected time when you book.

Who usually books this

People due for a yearly checkup, anyone monitoring diabetes, thyroid or cholesterol, and patients sent by a doctor for a specific test.

Test lists can change with the referral. The centre confirms availability, preparation and fees when you book.
