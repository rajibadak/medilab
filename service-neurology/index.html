Neurology

Tests and consultation support for headache, seizure, nerve and related concerns.

What this service covers

Neurology services bring together EEG and a specialist consultation. The doctor decides which test is useful. Bring a list of current medicines and any earlier brain scans or prescriptions.

Tests and studies

  • Electroencephalogram (EEG)
  • Neurology consultation
  • Coordination with imaging when a scan is advised
  • Review of earlier reports

How to prepare

Hair should be clean and free of oil or spray before an EEG. Sleep may be restricted only if the doctor asks for a sleep-deprived study. Take regular medicines unless you are told to hold them.

Reports

The consultation note is given at the visit. EEG reporting time is confirmed when the test is booked.

Who usually books this

People with repeated headache, blackout, seizure, tingling or a referral from another doctor.

Test lists can change with the referral. The centre confirms availability, preparation and fees when you book.
