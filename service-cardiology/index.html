Cardiology

Heart rhythm and structure checks, plus the blood tests doctors use for cardiac risk.

What this service covers

Cardiology diagnostics at EGRA Medilab look for rhythm changes, pumping function and cholesterol risk. These tests support a consultation. They do not replace emergency care. If you have chest pain, severe breathlessness or fainting, go to the nearest emergency service.

Tests and studies

  • Electrocardiogram (ECG)
  • Echocardiography
  • Lipid profile
  • Blood sugar with the heart panel
  • Doctor review of the cardiac reports

How to prepare

Wear a top that is easy to adjust for ECG leads. Echo usually needs no fasting. If a blood test is booked the same day, follow the fasting instruction given at booking.

Reports

ECG is reported during the visit. Echo and lab reports follow the time shared at the desk.

Who usually books this

Adults with blood pressure, diabetes, family history of heart disease, or symptoms their doctor wants checked.

Test lists can change with the referral. The centre confirms availability, preparation and fees when you book.
