Terms and conditions

What a request on this website does, and what it does not do.

Sending the booking form asks the centre to call you. It does not reserve a machine, a doctor or a home-collection slot until the desk confirms it.

Test names and timings on these pages describe the usual service. Availability and fees are confirmed at booking and can change. A prescription always overrides a general description.

Articles are general information for readers. They are not an emergency service and not personal medical advice. For urgent symptoms, contact local emergency care.

You are responsible for the phone number you type. A wrong number means the confirmation call cannot reach you.
