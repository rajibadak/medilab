Your reports, ready to open

Routine reports are shared digitally so you can view them on a phone or computer and forward them to your doctor.

How to get a report

  • Keep the phone number you used at booking. The report alert goes there.
  • At the centre, the desk can resend a link or print a copy after an identity check.
  • Home-collection reports use the same process as centre visits.
  • For another person’s report, the desk needs that patient’s permission.

There is no separate public password on this website yet. Use the request form or call +91 98765 43210 and quote the patient name and bill or visit date. The team sends the file after confirming who is asking.

Do not post report files in open social chats. They contain personal health information.
