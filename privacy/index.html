Privacy policy

How this website handles the details you type into a form.

EGRA Medilab Diagnosis uses the name, phone number, email and note you submit to reply about a booking, a home collection or a report. Those details are for the centre’s desk. They are not sold.

Health reports are released only after the desk checks that the requester is the patient or has the patient’s permission. Do not send a report password or a full report file to an address you do not recognise.

Email newsletter requests store the address you typed so the centre can send health updates. You can ask to stop those messages by calling +91 98765 43210 or writing to info@egramedilabdiagnosis.com.

This is a plain notice for the website. It is not a substitute for the consent the centre takes when a sample is collected.
